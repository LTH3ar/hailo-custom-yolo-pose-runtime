Student moments: focused or distracted?

How to label

Look at the marked student across the three frames (about 2 seconds) and judge their attention during the lesson. You don't see what the system decided.

  • Focused: looking at the board or teacher, reading or writing class material, raising a hand, or briefly glancing then returning.
  • Distracted: turned away or talking to a neighbour, looking somewhere unrelated, using a phone, head down on the desk, for most of the 2 seconds.
  • Can't tell: face and body too hidden or too small to judge, or the box is not on one student.

Keys: 1 focused, 2 distracted, 3 can't tell, ← back, → next. Answers are kept in this browser; export them when done, or any time.
