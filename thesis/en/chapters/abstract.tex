% Abstract (edited directly)
Camera-based attention monitoring in schools has sparked strong objections where it recognized students' faces, sent footage to servers and scored individual students. This thesis check whether the useful part, a class-level indicator of how many students appear distracted, can be built without that level of surveillance and only with local data processing on the edge devices.

The system proposed by this thesis, hcpose, runs entirely on a Raspberry Pi 5 with a Hailo-8 accelerator. A YOLOv8-pose model reduced to 13 upper-body keypoints finds the students with no face is recognized, and frames stay in memory, and students exist only as anonymous seats.Using per-seat calibration, two head-turn measures and smoothed rules to decide whether each seat looks focused. The teachers see only the share of the class that appears distracted, with a 95\% Wilson interval.

On the edge device, the deployment setting using yolo v8 n model, with tiling of the far rows to runs in real time at 30 FPS and 47 ms latency. It draws about 4.1 W at the board plus about 2.5 W for the accelerator, without thermal throttling. Tiling raises the number of students found on the SCB dataset from 55\% to 72\%, and from 25\% to 66\% in the far third, without retraining. On COCO val2017, the deployed INT8 models lose 4-5 keypoint AP points against their PyTorch versions.

Classifiers trained on SCB do not transfer to real lessons well since they flag 50-82\% of an attentive lecture as distracted, this was attributed to the lack of data to train the models; adding a second dataset with the missing head-up attentive class (ClaBehavior) lowers it only to 44-51\%. The calibrated rules keep false alarms low, with 89-95\% specificity on 451 human labels from three clips, including a 10-student class recorded after every threshold was fixed. With a nose-to-shoulder head-turn measure that keeps working in profile, they find 56\% of scripted distraction but Phone use remains invisible to detection. The results show that an aggregate, on-device indicator is feasible, while reliable per-student judgement needs more classroom-specific training data.

\textbf{Keywords:} student engagement, pose estimation, edge computing, privacy by design, Hailo-8, Raspberry Pi
